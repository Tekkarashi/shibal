Android 7 or newer · Latest version on GitHub Releases

Install in a minute

  1. Download on your phoneOpen this page on your Android phone and tap Download Shibal.
  2. Open the APKAndroid asks to allow installs from your browser. Allow it for this install, then tap Install.
  3. Allow notificationsOpen Shibal and allow notifications so reminders can reach you.
  4. Check it worksSettings → Send a test, then lock your phone. If the test reminder arrives, you’re set.

What it does

Share it inFrom WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, X or Chrome, tap Share and pick Save to Shibal. It spots the deadline, and keeps flyers with the item.
Reminders that landUses Android’s alarm-clock path for last-day alerts, so battery savers can’t swallow them.
Sorted by urgencyMissed, today, next 3 days, this week, later. Swipe right when you’ve applied.
Yours aloneEverything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Questions

Is Shibal free?

Yes. It’s free and open source (MIT), with no ads and no account.

Why does Android warn about an unknown app?

Shibal installs straight from its GitHub releases instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm. The full source code is public on GitHub.

Does it need internet or a server?

No. Your items stay on your phone and Android schedules the reminders itself, so they arrive even offline.

I can’t find Shibal when sharing

Look for Save to Shibal in the share sheet, or type “Shibal” in its search box. After you use it a couple of times Android moves it to the front. Shibal accepts links, text, images (flyers) and WhatsApp chat exports.

How do I update?

Download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your saved items stay.

Reminders arrive late on my Tecno, Infinix or itel phone

In Shibal’s Settings, open Battery and set Shibal to unrestricted, allow auto-start, and lock it in recent apps. dontkillmyapp.com has steps for each brand.
