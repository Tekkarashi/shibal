## What's new

- **Reminders that keep going.** Every day from a week out, three times a day for the last two days, then on the day itself, until you mark it applied or skip it.
- **Shibal reads flyers.** Share a flyer or screenshot and Shibal reads it on your phone. If it says "apply by" or "deadline" next to a date, the date is filled in for you to check. Nothing is uploaded.
- **It checks the application page too.** When the message has no date, Shibal looks on the link's page for the deadline.
- **More ways of writing a date**, like "apply before Friday", "closes in 2 weeks" or "Apply by 30/11".
- When no deadline turns up, the form says so and gives you a quick way to open the link or the flyer and check yourself.
