## What's new

- **Reminders you can count on.** Shibal sets them again each time you open it, so any your phone dropped come back. They are set even before you allow notifications.
- **Shares no longer get lost** when your phone had closed Shibal in the background.
- **Settings shows your next reminder**, and warns you if the "Deadline reminders" category is switched off.
- If your saved list ever can't be read, a copy is kept aside instead of being written over.
